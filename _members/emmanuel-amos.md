Emmanuel is a first-year MS student working on embodied intelligence and optimization. His research focuses on the optimization of the co-design of robotic end-effectors, the products they handle, and the control policies that tie them together. More broadly, he is interested in AI-driven robotic systems that learn from interaction and adapt their behavior, with particular interests in learning-based control, adaptive systems, and expressive human–robot interaction.

Outside of research, he plays basketball, lifts weights, and cycles around the city. He also enjoys reading webnovels and manga, and playing competitive video games.

Frederike is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, with a courtesy appointment in the Robotics Institute. Her research combines global optimization and artificial intelligence to make robots more capable and trustworthy, with a focus on sample-efficient learning and reliable solutions to challenging nonconvex problems.
